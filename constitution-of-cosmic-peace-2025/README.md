## About the Publication
**The Constitution of Cosmic Peace – 2025** is an independently authored international peace and space-governance proposal concerned with the peaceful, ethical and sustainable governance of outer space.
The underlying PDF is internally titled:
**INTERNATIONAL SPACE LAW CONSTITUTION – 2025**
with the subtitle:
**A Charter for Cosmic Peace, Ethical Governance, and Continuity of Life.**
The document presents a moral, legal and policy-oriented framework addressing peaceful uses of outer space, non-militarization, environmental responsibility, artificial intelligence, privacy, scientific cooperation, equitable access, dispute settlement and the continuity of life.
The ethical concept of **Aram (Righteousness)** forms a central part of its philosophical framework.

## Proposed Space Security Force Prohibition
The historical document contains a dedicated provision titled:
**Article III-A — Universal Ban on Space Security Forces  (Moral and Legal Prohibition on Armed Cosmic Entities)**
The provision proposes restrictions concerning organized armed or military-structured formations operating in outer space or on celestial bodies.
It also discusses humanitarian rescue, collision avoidance, emergency response, environmental protection, space-traffic management, transparency, demilitarization and peaceful confidence-building.
These provisions are preserved here as proposals contained in the author's 2025 instrument.
Their presence in the document does not establish that they constitute existing binding international law.

## Proposed International Space Peace Council
The document proposes an **International Space Peace Council (ISPC)** as part of its institutional architecture.
The proposed functions described in the document include:
- monitoring implementation;- registration and certification of peaceful missions;- transparency and reporting;- cooperation with relevant international bodies;- review of alleged non-compliance;- peaceful dispute-resolution functions; and- environmental and ethical oversight.
The ISPC is a proposal contained in the independently authored instrument.
This archival record does not represent that the United Nations has established such a body or conferred institutional authority upon it.
