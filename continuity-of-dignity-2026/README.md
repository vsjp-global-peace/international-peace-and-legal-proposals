## About the Document

*THE CONTINUITY OF DIGNITY — AN ORDINARY HUMAN BEING’S QUESTION TO THE WORLD* is an independent, peaceful, non-binding and good-faith civil-society ethical–legal and philosophical Appeal.

The document addresses:

- Human dignity and equality
- Disability inclusion and practical accessibility
- Dignified ageing
- Continuity of care
- Responsible public service
- Prevention of avoidable humiliation, exclusion and abandonment
- Responsible care where sustained dependence exists
- Intergenerational responsibility
- Future generations
- Peaceful coexistence
- Continuity with Aram
