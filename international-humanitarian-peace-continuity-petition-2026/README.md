## About the Petition

The **International Humanitarian Peace and Continuity Petition** is an independently authored humanitarian and constitutional public-interest proposal concerning peaceful international coexistence, humanitarian mediation, ecological continuity, responsible technology, intergenerational responsibility and the long-term continuity of humanity and Earth.

The underlying publication is formally titled:

> **Official International Petition on the Establishment of a Peace-Oriented, Independent, Humanitarian, and Civilizational International Mediation and Continuity Framework for Global Continuity, Non-Violence, Ecological Responsibility, Intergenerational Security, Humanitarian Coexistence, and the Dignified Continuity of All Beings**

The document is presented as a peaceful framework intended to encourage reflection on neutral humanitarian mediation, coexistence-centred international dialogue and responsible civilizational continuity.

It does not claim sovereign authority, governmental status, diplomatic privilege or coercive power.
