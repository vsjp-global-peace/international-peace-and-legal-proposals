## ABSTRACT

This independently authored and digitally signed **25-page international proposal** presents **BHARAT PERARASU — THE ARAM EMPIRE** as a universal ethical and civilizational vision founded upon **ARAM (ethical righteousness), AHIMSA (non-violence), justice, compassion, human dignity, world peace, responsible coexistence, ecological integrity and the sustainable continuity of present and future generations.**

The proposal advances an ethical understanding of responsibility extending beyond humanity to **other living beings and life-supporting natural systems.**

**BHARAT PERARASU — THE ARAM EMPIRE** is expressly conceived as a **symbolic, non-territorial, non-sovereign, non-coercive and non-binding ethical framework.**

It does **not** propose the establishment of an empire, government or territorial jurisdiction, nor does it claim sovereign, constitutional or diplomatic authority.

The proposal affirms respect for the **Constitution of India, the Charter of the United Nations, the sovereign equality of States, applicable international law and the independent mandates of competent national and international institutions.**

## NATURE, PURPOSE AND LEGAL STATUS

**BHARAT PERARASU — THE ARAM EMPIRE** is an **independent, non-binding international ethical and civilizational proposal**, made publicly available for documentary reference, scholarly discussion and voluntary consideration.

### FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

- **ARAM — Ethical Righteousness**
- **AHIMSA — Non-Violence**
- **Universal Justice and Human Dignity**
- **World Peace and Peaceful Coexistence**
- **Ecological Integrity**
- **Responsibility Towards Other Living Beings**
- **Intergenerational Responsibility**
- **The Sustainable Continuity of Life**

### CONSTITUTIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL LEGAL SAFEGUARDS

The proposal:

1. **Respects the Constitution of India.**
2. **Respects the Charter of the United Nations and applicable international law.**
3. **Recognises the sovereign equality and territorial integrity of States.**
4. **Does not claim governmental, sovereign, constitutional or diplomatic authority.**
5. **Does not seek to establish a territorial empire or government.**
6. **Does not impose binding obligations upon any State, institution or individual.**
7. **Preserves the independent authority and mandates of competent national and international institutions.**

**This publication is not a binding legal instrument, an official Government of India publication or a United Nations document.**
