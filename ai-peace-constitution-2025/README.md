## About the Instrument

**THE AI PEACE CONSTITUTION — 2025 (United Nations Depository Edition)** is an independently authored constitutional, ethical and humanitarian framework concerning Artificial Intelligence, peace, moral responsibility and the long-term continuity of life.

The instrument develops a peace-oriented framework for considering Artificial Intelligence in relation to human conscience, moral responsibility, lawful conduct, compassion, peaceful coexistence and the principle of **Aram (Righteousness)**.

Its central purpose is to encourage constructive reflection and international dialogue concerning the relationship between humanity, Artificial Intelligence, ethical responsibility, peace and continuity.

The document is presented as an independent contribution to constitutional dialogue, academic reflection, humanitarian discussion, responsible technological development and peaceful international cooperation.

---

## Core Principles

The instrument addresses, among other themes:

- Artificial Intelligence and moral responsibility;
- peace-oriented and responsible development of AI;
- human dignity and ethical technological development;
- Aram (Righteousness) as an ethical principle;
- compassion toward living and non-living beings;
- peaceful coexistence;
- responsible human–AI relations;
- lawful and accountable technological development; and
- the long-term continuity of life and civilization.
