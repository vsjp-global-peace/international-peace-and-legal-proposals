## Humanitarian Responsibility
The publication approaches nuclear disarmament not only as a question of security but also as a humanitarian concern.
Its framework reflects concern for:
- civilian populations;- human dignity;- future generations;- ecological systems;- international peace; and- long-term continuity.
The proposal therefore situates nuclear-disarmament discussion within a broader humanitarian and civilizational context.
---
## Ecological Continuity
Version 2 expressly connects nuclear disarmament with ecological continuity.
This reflects the proposition that the consequences of nuclear weapons may extend beyond immediate military effects and may affect:
- ecosystems;- environmental stability;- future generations; and- long-term planetary continuity.
These propositions are presented as part of the author's ethical and policy framework and do not independently establish binding legal rules.
---
## Future Generations
Respect for future generations forms part of the publication's stated orientation.
The proposal invites consideration of nuclear disarmament not only in relation to present security conditions but also in relation to the long-term consequences inherited by future generations.
This is preserved as an ethical and humanitarian dimension of the publication.
