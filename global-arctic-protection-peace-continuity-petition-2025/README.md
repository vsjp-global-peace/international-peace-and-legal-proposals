## About the Petition
The **Global Petition for the Protection of the Arctic and the Continuity, Dignity, and Peace of All Living and Non-Living Beings** is an independently authored moral, ethical, environmental and diplomatic petition concerning the protection of the Arctic, planetary climate stability, biodiversity, indigenous dignity, international peace, intergenerational responsibility and the continuity of living and non-living beings.
The historical Petition presents itself as:
- peaceful;- non-binding;- moral and ethical in character;- environmentally focused;- diplomatic and consultative;- submitted in good faith;- respectful of State sovereignty; and- intended to contribute to international dialogue and cooperative action.
The Petition is grounded in the author's ethical framework of **Aram (Righteousness)**, **Ahimsa (Non-Violence)**, environmental responsibility, peaceful cooperation and continuity.

## Constitutional Submission Context
The historical Petition was submitted in reference to **Article 51(c) of the Constitution of India**.
The covering representation was addressed to the constitutional office of the President of the Republic of India and requested that, if considered appropriate, the Petition be transmitted through competent governmental and diplomatic channels, including the Ministry of External Affairs, for international consideration.
The Petition itself refers to possible consideration by bodies including:
- the United Nations;- the United Nations General Assembly;- the United Nations Security Council;- the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP);- UNESCO;- the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP);- the Arctic Council;- the International Maritime Organization (IMO); and- United Nations Member States.
The existence of the submission and registration records does not, by itself, establish governmental endorsement, diplomatic transmission, United Nations adoption or international approval.

## Purpose and Scope
The Petition seeks international reflection and cooperation concerning:
- protection of the Arctic environment;- climate stability;- biodiversity;- indigenous communities and dignity;- ecological continuity;- peaceful use of the Arctic;- restraint concerning militarization and strategic escalation;- responsible resource use;- intergenerational justice;- scientific cooperation; and- protection of the continuity and dignity of living and non-living beings.
The Petition presents Arctic protection as both an environmental concern and a question of global peace, planetary responsibility and long-term continuity.

## Principal Requests and Proposals
The historical Petition places several requests before relevant public and international institutions for consideration.
### 1. Global Peace and Continuity Zone
The Petition requests consideration of recognizing the Arctic as a **Global Peace and Continuity Zone**, with environmental preservation and peaceful use as central objectives.
### 2. Restraint and Peaceful Cooperation
The Petition encourages restraint concerning:
- military escalation;- aggressive strategic deployment;- environmentally harmful exploitation; and- activities that may threaten Arctic ecological stability.
These proposals are expressly framed within respect for international law and State sovereignty.
### 3. Protection of Arctic Life and Ecosystems
The Petition asks that the dignity and continuity of Arctic life and ecosystems receive greater ethical and international attention.
Examples identified in the historical document include:
- polar bears;- seals;- marine species;- fragile ecosystems; and- indigenous communities.
### 4. Proposed Arctic Peace and Continuity Mechanism
The principal Petition refers to a proposed:
**“Permanent Arctic Peace and Continuity Mission.”**
Its contemplated purposes include:
- protection of life;- environmental conservation;- scientific cooperation;- peace assurance; and- continuity of all beings.
Separate governmental-portal representations associated with the same Petition use the expression:
**“Permanent Arctic Peace and Continuity Centre.”**
This README preserves both formulations because they occur in different parts of the historical documentary record.
Neither formulation should be interpreted as evidence that such a Mission or Centre has been formally established, approved, funded or recognized by the Government of India, the United Nations or any other institution.
### 5. International Scientific and Peace Cooperation
The Petition also proposes international scientific cooperation and environmentally protective engagement concerning Arctic stability.
Any participation by States, international organizations or public institutions would remain subject to applicable international law, institutional competence and State consent.
