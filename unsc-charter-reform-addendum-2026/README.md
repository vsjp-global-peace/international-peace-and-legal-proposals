## Purpose

This Addendum forms part of the continuing VISHWA SHANTI JANATA PARTY (VSJP) documentary series concerning proposed reform of the United Nations Security Council.

Its principal purposes are to:

- preserve the original VSJP-specific institutional request advanced in Version 1;
- clarify that Version 2 does not withdraw, cancel, extinguish or supersede Version 1;
- preserve Version 2 as the broader universal, non-exclusive and peace-centred framework;
- distinguish existing international law from proposed future institutional reform;
- clarify relevant constitutional and international-law questions;
- identify a lawful United Nations Charter-reform pathway for future international consideration; and
- preserve documentary continuity among Version 1, Version 2 and Version 3.

## Legal Character

The document is an independent, peaceful, non-binding, prospective and legal-clarificatory reform proposal.

It does not claim:

- present United Nations Security Council membership;
- present Permanent Civilizational Membership;
- veto authority;
- sovereignty;
- diplomatic status;
- United Nations recognition;
- unilateral institutional entitlement; or
- authority superior to the Charter of the United Nations.

Any future institutional status proposed in the document remains subject to lawful United Nations Charter procedures, sovereign Member State consent and applicable international processes.
