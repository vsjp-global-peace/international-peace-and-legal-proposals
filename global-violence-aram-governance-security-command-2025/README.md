## About the Document

This archival record preserves the 2025 document titled:

**“United Nations Depository Submission – Global Violence, Aram-Based Governance, and Unified National Security Command (2025)”**

The internal document is headed:

**“International Statement on the Global Escalation of Violence and the Imperative for Aram-Based Governance and a Unified National Security Command Structure.”**

The document addresses global violence, peace, ethical governance, security coordination, human dignity, the welfare of security personnel and their families, and the protection and continuity of living and non-living beings.

It presents **Aram (righteousness)** as an ethical foundation for peace, justice, non-violence, responsible governance, and continuity.

## Principal Themes

The document discusses:

1. the prevention and reduction of violence;
2. Aram-based ethical governance;
3. national security coordination;
4. timely and effective access to justice;
5. international peace and peaceful cooperation;
6. protection of living beings and life-supporting systems;
7. human rights, dignity and welfare of security personnel and their families; and
8. international ethical-security dialogue and cooperation.

The document also proposes an annual international ethical-security cooperation forum and a coordinated national security structure as matters for consideration.
