## About the Ocean Constitution
The **Ocean Constitution – 2025** is an independently authored moral, environmental, scientific and legal-policy charter concerned with the protection of oceans, marine life, ecological continuity, environmental justice and peaceful international cooperation.
The historical document presents the ocean within a broad ethical framework concerned with:
- marine ecosystems;- biodiversity;- environmental protection;- intergenerational continuity;- ocean peace;- ecological responsibility;- marine governance;- Artificial Intelligence;- sustainable development; and- the dignity and continuity of living and non-living beings.
The document should be understood as an independently authored international policy and ethical proposal rather than as a binding international constitution or treaty.

These references form part of the author's legal, ethical and policy framework.
Their inclusion does not imply endorsement or adoption of the Ocean Constitution by the institutions associated with those instruments.

## Core Ethical Framework
The Ocean Constitution presents the ocean as more than an economic or territorial resource.
Its ethical framework emphasizes:
- protection of marine life;- ecological continuity;- intergenerational responsibility;- restraint in exploitation;- prevention of environmental destruction;- peaceful use of ocean spaces;- environmental justice;- scientific responsibility; and- moral responsibility toward living and non-living systems.
The document seeks to integrate environmental ethics with international cooperation and long-term continuity.
