## About the Charter

**The Animal Constitution – 2025 (United Nations Depository Edition)** is an independently authored moral, ethical and policy-oriented charter concerning the recognition, protection, dignity and continuity of non-human living beings.

The PDF itself is titled:

**INTERNATIONAL CHARTER FOR THE RECOGNITION AND PROTECTION OF NON-HUMAN LIVING BEINGS**

The Charter invokes the ethical principle of **Aram (Moral Righteousness)** and addresses the treatment of animals and other non-human forms of life through a proposed international framework centred on compassion, coexistence, habitat protection, sentience, ethical treatment and continuity.

The historical PDF is preserved in this repository without alteration. This README provides a current public documentary and archival description of that historical record.

---

## Principal Areas Addressed

The Charter proposes consideration of principles and measures concerning:

- recognition and protection of non-human living beings;
- ethical consideration of sentience;
- prevention of cruelty, exploitation and commodification;
- habitat and food security;
- ethical oversight of research involving living beings;
- reform of entertainment and commercial practices involving animals;
- education concerning compassion and coexistence;
- integration of non-human protection into public policy;
- international monitoring and cooperation; and
- continuity of life as an ethical and civilizational concern.

These provisions constitute proposals contained within the independently authored Charter and should not be interpreted as statements of existing international legal obligations unless independently established by applicable law.

## Proposed Institutional Framework

The Charter proposes an:

**Independent International Council for Non-Human Justice (ICNHJ)**

as a possible future international monitoring, oversight and cooperation mechanism concerned with the protection of non-human beings.

References in the Charter to the proposed ICNHJ, compliance mechanisms, investigations, reparative action or recommendations describe elements of the author's proposed framework.

They do not establish that such an institution presently exists within the United Nations system or possesses any current international legal authority.
