## 1. Purpose and Central Principle

This repository directory preserves a public documentary record of an independently authored, peaceful, evidence-oriented and non-binding international policy framework concerning food safety, safe drinking water, human dignity, environmental integrity and the continuity of present and future generations.

The framework advances the principle that no human being should experience preventably inferior protection from food or drinking-water harm merely because of geography, nationality, poverty, political circumstances, social conditions or generation.

Universal equal protection does not require identical food, diets, water composition, biological needs, cultural traditions or national regulatory systems.

The framework also recognises an ethical responsibility toward non-human living beings dependent upon human care, while preserving the legal distinctions between human rights, animal welfare, environmental responsibilities and ethical stewardship.

## 2. Foundational Ethical Architecture

### LIFE — FOOD — PEACE — CONTINUITY — ARAM

The framework connects nourishment, safe drinking water, peace, ecological responsibility, human dignity and intergenerational continuity.

**ARAM** is presented as an ethical orientation toward righteousness, responsibility, non-harm, dignity and continuity, not as an independent source of international legal obligation.

---

## 3. Principal Policy Areas

The framework addresses:

1. Universal equal protection against preventable food and water harm.

2. Preventive food safety from source to consumption.

3. Scientific drinking-water safety and water-safety planning.

4. Prevention of intentional adulteration, fraudulent substitution and deliberate contamination.

5. Scientific distinctions between hazard, risk, exposure, uncertainty and established harm.

6. Environmental integrity, soil protection, biodiversity and pollution prevention.

7. Applicable humanitarian protections concerning food and water during armed conflict, disaster and displacement.

8. Protection of vulnerable populations and future generations.

9. A proposed cooperative global minimum protection baseline, subject to applicable law and competent institutional consideration.

10. National implementation, scientific monitoring, accountability, capacity-building and continuous improvement.
