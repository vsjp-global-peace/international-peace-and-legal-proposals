## About the Petition
The **International Petition for Ethical Science, Global Peace, Ecological Continuity, Responsible Artificial Intelligence, Intergenerational Responsibility, and the Establishment of a Global Aram Framework for Responsible Research (United Nations Depository Edition)** is an independently authored international public-interest proposal concerning ethical science, responsible research, global peace, ecological continuity, Artificial Intelligence, human dignity, scientific accountability, intergenerational responsibility and long-term global continuity.
The Zenodo publication describes the document as a **non-binding academic, humanitarian, constitutional, ethical and public-policy contribution** intended to encourage peaceful international dialogue.
The proposal addresses the relationship between scientific development, technological responsibility, humanitarian values, ecological stewardship, international cooperation, future generations and the long-term continuity of life and civilization.
