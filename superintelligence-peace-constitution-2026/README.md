## Purpose and Character
This repository preserves the digitally signed International Submission Edition — 2026 of **The World's First Identified Superintelligence Peace Constitution — For Humanity, World Peace and Peaceful Continuity**.
The instrument is an independent, peaceful, good-faith and non-binding civil-society constitutional proposal concerning the prospective governance of Superintelligence.
It seeks to contribute a preventive, corrective and peace-centred constitutional architecture directed toward:
- human dignity and internationally recognized human rights;- meaningful human agency and responsibility;- scientific integrity and responsible foresight;- Superintelligence safety, controllability and accountability;- catastrophic-risk prevention;- international peace and security;- non-weaponization and peaceful technological development;- responsible concern for life, biodiversity and ecosystems;- justice, due process and accountable public institutions;- children and future generations;- responsible innovation and global inclusion;- correction, learning and prevention of recurrence; and- long-term peaceful continuity.
The Constitution does not assert that Superintelligence presently exists, nor that its emergence is inevitable.

## Constitutional Direction
The constitutional orientation of the instrument may be summarized through the following principles:
**PEACE BEFORE POWER.**  **DIGNITY BEFORE MERE EFFICIENCY.**  **RESPONSIBILITY BEFORE CAPABILITY.**  **EVIDENCE BEFORE ASSERTION.**  **UNDERSTANDING BEFORE FEAR.**  **LAW BEFORE UNILATERAL POWER.**  **HUMAN AGENCY BEFORE AUTOMATED SOVEREIGNTY.**  **PREVENTION BEFORE IRREVERSIBLE HARM.**  **COOPERATION BEFORE DOMINATION.**  **CORRECTION BEFORE ENTRENCHMENT.**  **CONTINUITY BEFORE DESTRUCTION.**
Aram shall guide conscience.  Science shall inform responsible foresight.  Law shall govern legal obligation.  Human responsibility shall remain accountable for consequential human choice.  Peace shall remain the direction.  Continuity shall remain the responsibility.
