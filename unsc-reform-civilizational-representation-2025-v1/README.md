## About the Submission

The **Constitutional Civilizational Submission on United Nations Security Council Reform and Non-Violent Civilizational Representation (UN Depository Edition — 2025)** is an independently authored proposal concerning reform of the United Nations Security Council and the concept of peaceful, non-violent and life-centred civilizational representation within global peace-governance frameworks.

The original document is dated **27 November 2025** and is presented on its cover as an:

> **OFFICIAL INTERNATIONAL APPEAL**  
> *(Constitutional Submission under Article 51(c) of the Constitution of India)*

The document proposes structural reform of the United Nations Security Council and, as part of the author's proposal, advocates consideration of VSJP for permanent civilizational representation.

These propositions form part of the author's independently submitted proposal and must not be interpreted as having been accepted, adopted or endorsed by the United Nations, the Government of India or any other institution.
