## Abstract

The Universal Space Peace Constitution — 2026 is an independently authored, non-binding international constitutional proposal developed to encourage the peaceful exploration and responsible use of outer space, the prevention of armed conflict beyond Earth, ethical scientific advancement, ecological responsibility and the long-term continuity of existence.

Grounded in ARAM (ethical responsibility), AHIMSA (non-violence), human dignity, sovereign equality and intergenerational responsibility, the proposal advocates future international negotiations towards a comprehensive, universal and verifiable prohibition of weapons in outer space, subject to lawful agreement among States.

The document addresses the prevention of destructive anti-satellite activities, orbital debris, harmful contamination, responsible extraterrestrial resource utilization, planetary protection, peaceful scientific cooperation, responsible artificial intelligence, and safeguards for living beings and non-living natural systems.

It further advances a proposed Millennial Continuity Horizon from 2026 to 3026 and beyond, encouraging long-term responsibility towards present and future generations.

The proposal also includes an Indian Space Peace and Safety Initiative for consideration exclusively within the sovereign constitutional and governmental authority of the Republic of India.

## International Legal Position

This document respects the Charter of the United Nations, the 1967 Outer Space Treaty and applicable international law.

It distinguishes existing legal obligations from proposed future norms and does not purport to amend existing treaties or impose obligations upon States.

It is not an internationally adopted constitution, treaty, governmental instrument or legally binding agreement.

It claims no diplomatic authority, United Nations endorsement, recognition or institutional approval.
