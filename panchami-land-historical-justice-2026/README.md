- economic security;
- land access;
- social protection; and
- greater independence for historically marginalized communities.

These historical propositions are preserved here as part of the documentary account advanced by the publication.

## Historical Extent and Estimated Area

Annexure IV discusses the historical extent of Panchami Lands.

The document cites an estimate approaching:

> **approximately 1.2 million acres  
> (around 485,000 hectares)**

for land historically referenced as Panchami Lands in present-day Tamil Nadu.

This figure must be read together with the document's own express qualification that the precise historical extent remains uncertain because of factors including:

- incomplete archival records;
- changes in district boundaries;
- historical classification inconsistencies;
- subsequent administrative modifications; and
- partial loss of land registers.

Accordingly, this repository preserves the figure strictly as an **estimate cited by the publication**, not as an independently verified contemporary land total.

The document itself calls for archival and administrative verification.
