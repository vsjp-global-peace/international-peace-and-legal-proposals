## About the Petition

The **International Petition for the Global Prohibition of the Dehumanizing Term “Beggar” and for the Recognition of Humanity as “Sharers”** is an independently authored moral–linguistic and human-dignity initiative.

The petition examines the language used to describe persons experiencing poverty, deprivation or dependence upon the assistance of others and proposes a dignity-oriented reconsideration of terminology.

Its central proposal is the voluntary movement away from the term **“Beggar”** and the consideration of **“Sharers”** as a dignity-based alternative within the ethical and civilizational framework advanced by the author.

The initiative is presented for peaceful international dialogue concerning human dignity, equality, shared responsibility, language, ethics and social inclusion.

---

## Core Principle

The petition proceeds from the view that language can influence social perception, human relationships and the dignity with which persons are regarded.

It therefore encourages reflection on whether terminology associated with poverty or requests for assistance can be expressed in ways that better preserve human dignity and shared humanity.

The proposed concept of **“Sharers”** emphasizes interdependence, mutual responsibility and the understanding that giving and receiving form part of human social existence.
