## About the Instrument

**THE WORLD'S FIRST WORLD PEACE CONSTITUTION — 2026: ADVANCED CONCISE INTERNATIONAL SUBMISSION EDITION** is an independently authored, peaceful, good-faith and non-binding global constitutional and durable peace-solution proposal.

The instrument is intended to contribute constructively to world peace, human dignity, peaceful coexistence, responsible international cooperation, the continuity of life and life-supporting systems, the interests of present and future generations, and the responsible development and use of technology.

## Constitutional and International-Law Context

The submission to the Hon'ble President of the Republic of India respectfully invoked the constitutional spirit reflected in **Article 51(c)** and **Article 51(d)** of the Constitution of India, concerning, respectively, the fostering of respect for international law and treaty obligations and the encouragement of settlement of international disputes by arbitration.

The instrument is framed with respect for:

- the Charter of the United Nations;
- applicable international law;
- the sovereign equality of States;
- territorial integrity;
- political independence;
- non-intervention;
- peaceful settlement of international disputes;
- human rights; and
- India's constitutional and legal order.
