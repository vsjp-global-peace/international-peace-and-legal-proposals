## About the Petition

The **International Constitutional & Humanitarian Peace Petition — 2025** is an independently authored constitutional, humanitarian and international-law-oriented petition concerning international peace, non-violence, human dignity, continuity, ethical responsibility, Artificial Intelligence, and the well-being of persons engaged in sustained peace and humanitarian service.

The historical petition is addressed through the constitutional office of the President of the Republic of India for consideration and, where appropriate, engagement with:

- the United Nations;
- the international community of nations; and
- relevant global peace, human-rights and humanitarian institutions.

The petition is presented as a peaceful humanitarian appeal rather than as an assertion of governmental authority, diplomatic entitlement or institutional status.

---

## Constitutional Context

The historical document is framed in reference to **Article 51(c) of the Constitution of India**.

Article 51(c) concerns fostering respect for international law and treaty obligations in the dealings of organised peoples with one another.

The petition invokes that constitutional principle as part of its proposed route for international consideration.

The historical document also associates its purposes with peace and international cooperation. This GitHub record preserves that broader authorial framing while distinguishing it from the specific constitutional wording of Article 51(c).

The petition was submitted through the constitutional office of the President of India for consideration and possible transmission or engagement as deemed appropriate.

This does not, by itself, establish:

- endorsement by the President of India;
- personal review by the President;
- adoption by the Government of India;
- diplomatic transmission;
- United Nations receipt;
- institutional approval; or
- creation of any legal entitlement.

## Humanitarian Well-Being Dimension

A significant part of the historical petition concerns the importance of preserving the physical, emotional and mental well-being of persons engaged in sustained peace and humanitarian work.

The document requests consideration of reasonable and lawful supportive conditions that may help sustain long-term humanitarian and peace-oriented contribution.

The requests include consideration of:

- mental peace;
- personal security;
- dignified living conditions;
- rest;
- access to peaceful environments;
- restorative travel; and
- family well-being.

These requests are presented in the historical document as humanitarian considerations rather than as established legal entitlements.

This GitHub README intentionally summarizes those matters at a high level rather than reproducing unnecessary personal detail from the historical petition.

## Requests Contained in the Historical Petition

The document respectfully seeks consideration of matters including:

### 1. Peace and Humanitarian Recognition

Consideration of the value of sustained humanitarian and global-peace service within constitutional and international ethical frameworks.

### 2. Reasonable Supportive Conditions

Consideration of lawful and reasonable supportive measures capable of sustaining personal well-being and continued peace-oriented contribution.

### 3. Rest and Restorative Environment

Consideration of opportunities for rest, peaceful environments and restorative travel in connection with long-term humanitarian service.

### 4. Human Dignity of Peace Workers

Recognition, as an ethical proposition, that persons engaged in peace and humanitarian work remain entitled to human dignity, emotional balance, family life and well-being.

These are requests contained in an independently authored petition.

They do not create an automatic right to governmental support, funding, travel, security arrangements, institutional recognition or special treatment.
